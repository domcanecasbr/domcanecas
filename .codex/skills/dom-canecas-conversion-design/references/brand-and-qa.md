# Brand and QA reference

## Core tokens

- Paper: `#fffdf9`
- Warm surface: `#f7f1e8`
- Soft terracotta accent: `#b95132`
- Dark accent: `#7f321f`
- Ink: `#171310`
- Muted ink: `#625b55`
- Line: `#e5ddd2`

Use the accent for small emphasis, badges, icons, and interactive states. Keep primary WhatsApp and budget CTAs black unless campaign testing establishes a better alternative.

## Image direction

- Natural or soft studio light; believable ceramic glaze and print edges.
- Warm neutral surfaces and uncluttered compositions.
- Hero images should leave deliberate copy space or sit in a separate grid column.
- Do not render readable names, slogans, or third-party marks into generated images.
- Do not reuse one image repeatedly to simulate a gallery.

## Page review

- The first viewport includes a mug, a concrete promise, delivery scope, and WhatsApp CTA.
- Gift, company, and event paths are visually distinguishable.
- Buttons state the resulting action.
- Claims and testimonials are traceable to supplied evidence.
- Images have relevant alt text and stable dimensions.
- Header, menu, CTA, cookie controls, and footer remain usable by keyboard.
- Layout works at 320px, 390px, 768px, 1024px, and wide desktop.
- No content is hidden behind sticky or fixed UI.
- Existing analytics and consent events still fire under their original names.
- Metadata accurately describes the final page.
